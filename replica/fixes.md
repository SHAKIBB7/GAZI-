## 2. What Is Missing (Features Demanded by Name)

1. **Deterministic Local Fulfillment:** 24–48hr courier dispatch within Bangladesh (Dhaka inside 24h, outside Dhaka 48–72h) replacing 6–10 week international postal delays.
2. **Zero-Risk Cash on Delivery (COD) & Local Mobile Banking:** Support for bKash, Nagad, and physical cash upon doorstep inspection.
3. **Automated Tracking with SMS Updates:** Dedicated logistics tracking numbers (`GZ-TRK-...`) with real-time delivery stage updates.
4. **Professional Bat Knocking-In & Preparation Add-On:** 15,000-stroke automated mallet knocking, edge rounding, raw linseed oiling, and clear scuff sheet installation before bat shipment.
5. **Atomic Stock Reservation:** Prevention of ghost orders by executing concurrency-safe transactional inventory deductions at checkout.
6. **Instant Doorstep Size Replacement:** Hassle-free size exchanges handled directly by the delivery driver with zero return penalty fees.

## 3. What Is Unsolved (Whole Ignored Customer Segments)

- **The South Asian Cricket Community:** International retailers like Pro:Direct force UK/EU shoppers only. Bangladeshi cricketers, coaches, and clubs face exorbitant overseas shipping charges, currency exchange penalties, bank international transaction restrictions, customs duty shocks at Dhaka airport, and zero return recourse.
- **Grassroots Clubs & School Academies:** Western multi-sport giants cater to casual football and runners; they ignore cricket clubs requiring bulk equipment batches, standardized junior-to-senior sizing, and equipment durability warranties.
